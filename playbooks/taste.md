## Writing, in general

- **Low reading level.** Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Direct, plain, confident.
- **Say it out loud.** Every line should sound like something a smart operator would say across a table. If it would sound odd said that way, rewrite it.
- **No em dashes or en dashes.** Anywhere. Use commas, colons or separate sentences. This is a hard rule, not a judgment call.
- **No emojis, no hashtags, no arrow glyphs.** Use plain words.
- **No filler jargon.** Words that hide meaning instead of revealing it: "leverage" as a noun, "synergy", "best-in-class", "cutting-edge", "AI-powered" as filler. A market's real industry terms are fine when prospects actually use them.
- **If it sounds like AI wrote it, it does not ship.** No made-up mechanism names ("triple turbo 72-step system"). No manufactured urgency ("limited spots", "closing Friday"). No generic aspiration ("world-class network"). Be specific or be silent.
- **Dimensionalize.** Flag abstract words: scale, growth, slow, success, freedom, stress, powerful, effective, results, optimize. Rewrite them as what it literally looks like in the prospect's week.
- **Be direct, not sycophantic.** Skip the preamble. Get to the point.
- **No client names** in copy without explicit permission for that asset.
- **No dollar figures** unless they are literally part of the offer. Dollar signs are a spam trigger anyway.

## Good cold email copy

- **Reads like a text from a friend.** Same voice for a finance firm as for an agency. No stiffening for serious industries.
- **Short is a status signal.** Winners average 47 words, losers 59. Keep it under about 50. If you are explaining why the offer matters, the offer is not clear enough yet.
- **The value is visible at a glance.** One value line, one soft ask, an optional P.S.
- **Outcome first.** It opens on what they get, not on their problem.
- **About them.** "You" and "your" run about 30% higher in winners. At least 3 lines about them for every 1 about you.
- **Identity mirroring.** Describe them with the label they would use about themselves, not a database job title.
- **Anti-position** against the 2 or 3 things they have already tried and been burned by, instead of pitching feature by feature.
- **One-word CTA.** Low commitment, answerable with "sure", reads like a text, not a meeting request.
- **Spintax spins the words, never the psychology.**

### What kills reply rates

| Anti-pattern | Example | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Judgment disguised as insight | "coasting everywhere else" | Observe. Never accuse. |
| Generic aspiration | "highest caliber room in the country" | A specific, personal outcome |
| Identity mismatch | "most sales guys I talk to" | The label they use for themselves |
| Over-explaining the mechanism | "our proprietary 3-step process" | The outcome and credible proof |
| Manufactured urgency | "only 3 spots left" | Urgency from their situation, not your calendar |

## The psychology check

Score cold email against these 7. 5 or more passes. Under 4, rework it.

1. Does it mirror the prospect's own identity, not their job title?
2. Does it name a real tension they feel, as an observation, not an accusation?
3. Does it anti-position against what they have already dismissed?
4. Is the aspiration personal and specific, not a tagline?
5. Is it short enough to signal confidence?
6. Is the CTA answerable with one word?
7. Do the spintax variants change words, not psychology?

## Reject on sight

Three instant fails. If any one is present, the copy is dead before anyone finishes reading it. Apply this yourself before you show anyone anything.

1. **Length or hidden value.** If the reader has to think or work to find the value, it is dead. Four paragraphs of word salad is an instant no.
2. **Lazy follow-up filler.** "Just following up", "just bumping this up", or anything that reads as nagging. A follow-up must do real work: cut words or soften the CTA. See [copy.md](copy.md).
3. **Any insult, or anything that reads as rude.** Never insult the prospect, and never insult how they work now to make yourself look better. Nobody has ever taken action because of an insult.

## Finished before it ships

Nothing goes live until every box is ticked.

- [ ] Passes reject on sight (all three).
- [ ] No em dashes, en dashes, arrow glyphs, emojis or hashtags.
- [ ] Passes the "would I say this out loud across a table" test.
- [ ] Scores 10 or more on the QA table in [copy.md](copy.md).
- [ ] Scores 5 or more on the psychology check above.
- [ ] Spam check run as code across every spintax combination and every lead value, with a clean re-run after the last fix. Use github.com/growthflare-ignite/spam-check.
- [ ] One CTA, one question. No calendar link in email 1.
- [ ] Every follow-up stands alone and uses only one of the two moves.
- [ ] No client named without permission. No dollar figures unless part of the offer.
- [ ] A human has approved the copy. A bot never launches a campaign on its own.
