# Campaign Room

A group chat, not a Bot: the Head of GTM and the 5 specialists in 1 room, so a brief, a list, a draft and a build can be handed over without you relaying it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Members | Head of GTM, Market Researcher, List Builder, Copywriter, Infrastructure & Deliverability, Inbox Manager |
| Rule | Short pointers only. Name the client and campaign code in every message. The work lives in files in `clients/` on your Mac. |

Create it in Grok Bot as a group chat with these 6 members, or let the [setup prompt](../setup-prompt.md) do it.
